About

Where psychology meets technology, policy and development

“Behavioural insight becomes powerful when it improves real lives and institutions.”

Trained at Obafemi Awolowo University with bachelor's, master's and doctoral degrees in Psychology, Dr. Ilevbare has carried behavioural science into innovation management, entrepreneurship, health research and public policy.

At NACETEM, she contributed to the documentation and validation of Nigerian indigenous knowledge as a pathway to sustainable development. Through the NISER Actualising Behaviour Change series, her work applies behavioural science to governance and anti-corruption.

Today, as Senior Lecturer and Director of the Entrepreneurship Skills and Innovation Centre at the University of Ilesa, she combines teaching, research, mentorship and innovation leadership.
